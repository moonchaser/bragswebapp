TrialCarta: clinical trial research, checked against the source

TrialCarta is a product built on the bRAGs engine for biotech teams. It searches ClinicalTrials.gov and PubMed, charts the trial landscape, compares a confidential protocol with competing trials, and checks every number against the registry record or paper it came from, all on your own computer.
